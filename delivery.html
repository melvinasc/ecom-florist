Good to Know

Eligibility & FAQs

What counts as "same-day eligible"?

Products marked with a green "Same-Day" badge on the shop and product pages can be ordered and delivered on the same calendar day, provided your order is placed before your zone's cut-off time.

What happens if I order after the cut-off?

No problem — your order will simply be scheduled for the next available delivery day. You'll be able to choose the exact date at checkout.

Can I choose a specific delivery time?

We deliver within business hours (9:00 AM – 6:00 PM). While we can't guarantee an exact hour, you can leave delivery instructions at checkout.

Do you deliver on weekends and public holidays?

Yes, we deliver 7 days a week including most public holidays, though cut-off times may be earlier — check the calendar at checkout for exact times.
