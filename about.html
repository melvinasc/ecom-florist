Our Story

Family-run, locally rooted, and obsessed with fresh flowers since day one.

Since 2014

Grown from a single flower cart

Bloom & Co. started as a small flower cart on a Sydney street corner. Twelve years later, we're a full-service florist delivering across the city — but our promise hasn't changed: hand-tied, farm-fresh flowers, delivered with genuine care.

Every bouquet is still arranged by hand in our studio each morning, using stems sourced from growers we know personally.
